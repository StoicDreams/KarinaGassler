A Personal Pursuit of Beauty

Maison Gassler began as a personal pursuit of beauty in everyday life, a place to share my passion for interiors, textiles and pattern, and the timeless ingredients that make a house feel like home.

Meet Karina

I'm Karina Gassler, a designer and 3D artist. I spend my days turning design visions into vivid renders, and my evenings sketching patterns, cutting hydrangeas from the garden, and slowly bringing our own home to life.

Maison Gassler is where those pieces come together: the patterns I design, the rooms we renovate, and the places and seasons that inspire them.
